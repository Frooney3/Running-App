Running tips

  • Most runs should feel easy. You should be able to chat while you run.
  • Add no more than about 10% to your weekly distance each week.
  • Rest days are when your body adapts. Don't skip them.
  • Comfortable, well-fitted shoes matter more than expensive ones.
  • Something sharp or persistent hurting? Stop and rest it.
